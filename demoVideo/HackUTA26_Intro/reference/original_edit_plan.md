## 2. The 12-second ambush: cut-by-cut

Rule: **never hold a shot over 1.5s.** Alternate Original (O) → Recreation (R), and make each R shot match the framing of the O shot before it, so the cut reads as the same scene.

## 3. Pitch hook (0:12–0:18)

- Freeze holds ~0.5s, record scratch, music cuts to silence.
- Teammate (still cornered) slowly turns to camera, deadpan. Push the fist aside, step out of frame-lock.
- Line: *"Want to learn self-defense?"* Then title card / game logo smash cut with the game's theme music (this is where your own audio takes over).
- Optional gag: attackers stay frozen in the background, one awkwardly lowers their fist.

## 4. Filming the recreation (checklist)

- **Look:** night, one cool blue/purple practical light behind the actors, fog via phone-flashlight in haze/vape-free options (steam from a kettle, or add fog in post). Crush the blacks.
- **Costumes:** black long-sleeve + white tape/duct tape bones, or print a skeleton hoodie. White face paint or a white balaclava is the signature; a **phone showing a skeleton face** works as a backup.
- **Camera:** shoot 24/30fps, 16:9, handheld, low angles. Shoot every R shot **twice**: one exact match, one funny variation.
- **Shoot coverage for each beat:** the wide + the close-up from §2 (6 setups total). The whole skit is shot in ~30 minutes.
- **Safety:** haymaker is faked with forced perspective, so the fist passes *past* the face, nothing lands. Do the punch on a slight diagonal toward camera. Clear the ground at the fence/wall.
- **Sound:** record clean footsteps and cloth/fence rattles as Foley; they'll replace the original audio.

## 5. The end payoff (callback, ~5s at ~1:35)

Return to the frozen frame. Teammate now uses a move from your game (block + redirect), attacker flies off. Hard cut to **09_miyagi_flying_kick** for 1s, then to your end card. It rewards the audience that remembered the opener and shows the self-defense moves working.

## 6. Grading and finishing

- Grade R footage to match O: cut saturation ~30%, push shadows teal/blue, add subtle grain and a light vignette.
- Add 1.85:1 letterbox bars during the skit only, and remove them at the pitch moment (a "breaking the fourth wall" cue).
- Add small VHS/80s timecode or "1984" lower-third on the first original shot to clearly signal the homage.

## 7. Audio and copyright (important)

- Original audio from the film is **copyrighted**. Devpost videos are usually hosted on YouTube/Vimeo, where Content ID can mute or block it. Keep **original-clip audio to under ~6s**, or **mute it and score the whole intro with your own 80s-style synth** (royalty-free: Pixabay/Uppbeat, or generate your own).
- The short clips are used for parody/homage, which tends to hold up, but I'm not a lawyer. Upload unlisted first to test before the deadline.

## 8. Edit workflow

1. Import the 9 clips.
2. Lay out the O shots on V1 per §2 timecodes, leaving gaps for R shots.
3. Film R shots, drop in, and trim to match action.
4. Add synth bed, SFX (record scratch, fist whoosh), then freeze frame.
5. Grade, add letterbox, export 1080p H.264.
